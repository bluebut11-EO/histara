Privacy Policy — HaJunNa Uploader

Last updated: October 22, 2026

HaJunNa Uploader is a personal program used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @HaJunNa, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to other people.

Google data the app uses

  • YouTube (youtube.upload, youtube): to upload the owner's videos as private, add them to the owner's playlist, and read the owner's channel name and video statistics.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): to read average view percentage and view duration for the owner's own videos.
  • Blogger (blogger): to save draft posts on, and list published posts from, the owner's own blog.

Storage and sharing

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Video statistics are shown to the owner in the owner's own dashboard. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers or any other Google users.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the token file from the computer.

Contact

Through the YouTube channel @HaJunNa.
