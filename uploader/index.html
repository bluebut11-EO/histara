HaJunNa Uploader

A personal tool for the YouTube channel @HaJunNa

HaJunNa Uploader is a private, single-user program that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's YouTube channel as private (the owner reviews and publishes them by hand).
  • Reads view, like and comment counts, and average view percentage, for those videos so the owner can see how they did.
  • Saves the matching blog post as a draft on the owner's own Blogger blog (the owner publishes it by hand).

Privacy policy: /uploader/privacy.html
